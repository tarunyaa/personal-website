The
Backside

What if a chip’s surface became quieter by moving its power underneath?

Backside power delivery separates power from signal routing. Pull the chip apart, switch the network, and watch one kind of complexity disappear while another takes its place.

The mechanism

Separate the networks, reorganize the surface.

Thin

The finished wafer is thinned until the transistors can be reached from below.

Connect

Nanoscale vertical vias carry current from backside metal into the transistor plane.

Release

Power leaves the crowded front metal stack, freeing routes for signals and clocks.

The gain

Shorter, wider power paths

Feeding power from below can reduce resistance and voltage droop while giving signals more room above.

The exchange

Geometric calm, process difficulty

Routing becomes cleaner, but wafer thinning, via alignment, thermal behavior, and test access become harder.

The idea

Complexity is not removed

It is displaced into a layer where it interferes less with the system’s primary work.
